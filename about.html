From engineering to markets

Pahal Jain is an Automotive Engineering graduate from Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU). Early in his career he worked with Volvo Eicher, then moved into customer-facing roles at Tata Sky and ICICI Bank — experiences that sharpened his understanding of people, process, and trust.

After COVID, he returned to Durg, Chhattisgarh, and founded Pahal Investment in July 2020 with a simple idea: help people take a confident first step toward a better financial tomorrow — एक बेहतर कल की पहल.

How we work

The practice blends classic charting with modern tooling. Pahal works with Renko, Candlestick, and Point & Figure (P&F) frameworks, and uses Streak-style automation to keep analysis repeatable and less emotional. The broader theme is combining practical broking support with thoughtful use of AI-assisted research — always keeping the client conversation human and clear.

Core focus areas include momentum investing, SIP planning, trading support, stock analysis, portfolio planning, and multi-asset guidance. There are no invented SEBI registration numbers or guaranteed-return claims on this site — markets carry risk, and decisions remain yours.
